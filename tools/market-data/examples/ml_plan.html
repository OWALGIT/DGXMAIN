03

Where the profit actually hides

Do
  • Cross-sectional, not index timing — rank 503 stocks by expected relative return.
  • Multi-horizon (5/20-day) & vol-targeted labels — smoother, more predictable than 1-day sign.
  • Regime-condition on VIX / credit (HYG) / curve — the baseline said these matter.
  • Alt-data that survived the bootstrap: attention (Margin-call/Recession lookups), shipping (Red Sea → credit).
  • Ensemble GBM + fine-tuned TS foundation; let RL size the bets.

The bootstrap discipline from the butterfly study carries in: with thousands of features × horizons, most "edges" are mirages. Every model earns its keep on out-of-sample, cost-aware returns — or it's cut.
